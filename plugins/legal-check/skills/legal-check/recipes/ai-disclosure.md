# Recipe: AI disclosure (EU AI Act Art. 50, California BOT Act)

Goal: people know when they're talking to an AI, and AI-generated media isn't passed off
as real.

## Done when
- Chat UIs say plainly near the input or in the first message: "You're chatting with an
  AI assistant. It can make mistakes." and label replies ("AI").
- System prompts never tell the model to deny being an AI or to claim to be human; if a
  persona name is used, it's presented as an AI persona.
- AI-written content shown as reviews, testimonials, or quotes from people is removed or
  clearly labelled (see `claims` recipe); AI-generated images/video of realistic people or
  events are labelled "AI-generated".
- Marketing copy doesn't say "no AI"/"human" when an AI does the work.

## Stack notes
Pure copy + prompt changes in any stack; in mobile apps put the notice on the chat
screen. Keep the disclosure visible, not only in Terms.
