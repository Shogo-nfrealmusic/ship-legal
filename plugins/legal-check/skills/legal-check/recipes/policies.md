# Recipe: privacy policy and terms (CalOPPA, CCPA content, liability terms)

Goal: pages exist, are linked from every page (and inside mobile apps), and describe what
the code actually does. They are drafts until a lawyer reviews them.

## Done when
- Privacy policy (`/privacy`) built from facts in the code: what is collected at sign-up
  and by forms, each third party named (analytics, replay, ad pixels, email/SMS,
  payments, storage, AI APIs), why, cookies/trackers and how to decline, session
  recording (if any), children ("not directed to children under 13"), how to access,
  delete, or export data, a California section (categories collected, sources, purposes,
  who receives them, rights to know/delete/correct/opt out, how to use them, whether you
  "sell" or "share" — ad pixels usually count as sharing), contact, effective date, and
  "updated at least every 12 months". Unknowns → `TODO(legal-check): ...`.
- Terms (`/terms`): who may use it (13+ if an age screen exists), acceptable use, user
  content license and removal, subscriptions/refunds summary if billing exists, link to
  /dmca if uploads exist, "as is" warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability,
  governing-law placeholder.
- Sign-up / checkout shows "By continuing you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy" with
  links next to the button (clickwrap), not only a footer link.
- Footer links to /privacy and /terms (and /dmca, "Your Privacy Choices" when those exist)
  on every page; mobile apps link to them from settings and sign-up.
- A top-of-file comment: "DRAFT generated by /legal-check. Have a lawyer review before
  relying on it." Never state facts you don't know (company name, address, retention,
  jurisdiction).

## Stack notes
Use the same routing idioms as the dmca recipe (file-based route, React Router route,
static .html, template + route, or an expo-router screen). For Expo, the App Store and
Play listings also need a public privacy-policy URL: Human TODO to host the page.
